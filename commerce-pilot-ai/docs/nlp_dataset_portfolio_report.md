## Best available portfolio -- explicit answers

**What datasets should CommercePilot use for future NLP research?**
Tier A: Amazon Appliances (English e-commerce methodology baseline, already approved) and ADAB (once its official release channel is confirmed -- CC BY 4.0, explicit e-commerce/customer-service domain, includes Egyptian dialect, very recent and credible). Tier B: LABR and ASTD (both acquired, hashed, GPLv2-licensed, genuine Arabic text) as general Arabic-methodology supports, and the Egyptian Tweets 40K corpus once Harvard Dataverse access is recovered.

**What datasets should remain quarantine?**
HARD (no license found + unreviewed Booking.com source-platform terms + contradictory sample-count figures), Jumia Egypt Reviews/JERD (topically ideal but LEVEL_5 unverified provenance), Egyptian Companies Reviews (provenance never established), the 2.5M-row Egyptian dialect collection (student project, domain mismatch).

**What datasets should be rejected?**
The originally-named 'EESA' and 'Arabic Feedback Corpus' (no matching dataset identified under those names -- not substituted with a look-alike), the ~323K Egyptian e-commerce corpus (paper never located despite dedicated search), the 2.5M-row dialect collection (domain mismatch, low priority).

**What important Egyptian channels remain uncovered?**
WhatsApp support, live chat, call-center transcripts (only paid commercial vendor options identified, not acquired), return/refund/cancellation/COD-refusal reason text, courier/delivery-exception feedback, product-quality/seller-escalation complaints, and any complaint/intent-labeled corpus at all -- see `docs/nlp_egyptian_first_party_data_gap_analysis.md`.

**What future first-party Egyptian data is required?**
See the schema specification in `docs/nlp_egyptian_first_party_data_gap_analysis.md`. Public datasets, even the best ones found this session (ADAB, Egyptian Tweets 40K), cannot substitute for first-party operational data tied to real orders, sellers, and support interactions under a documented consent basis.
