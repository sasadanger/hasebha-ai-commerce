# Olist Phase 2B Feature Availability Audit

No Contract B feature has repository evidence proving immutable availability at approval. The primary as-of whitelist is therefore empty.

The repository explicitly authorizes a separate retrospective snapshot-assumption sensitivity. Deterministically aggregated payment, item/value, and product physical-property features are admitted only to that analysis. Customer/seller geography and distance are excluded because snapshot and privacy-compatible representation are unresolved. Historical customer, seller, product, and regional outcome statistics are excluded because safe event-time construction and minimum-history policies are not established. Promised lead time, lifecycle status, shipping/handoff, actual/estimated delivery inputs, reviews, identifiers, and outcome aggregates remain excluded or forbidden.

The complete machine-readable classifications, sources, aggregation rules, risks, missingness observations, and whitelist hash are generated before training in `reports/generated/olist/phase2b/feature_availability_audit.json`.
