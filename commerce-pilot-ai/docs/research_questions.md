# Research Questions

These questions define measurable directions for future research without assuming dataset schemas, achievable results, or target values. Metrics, baselines, splits, and acceptance thresholds will be selected only after source and data validation. Every research output must map to a future Decision Action Card.

## Fulfillment and delivery intelligence

| Future research question | Measurement approach to define after validation | Future Decision Action Card |
|---|---|---|
| Can delivery outcomes be characterized consistently enough to surface operational patterns that warrant investigation? | Define eligible outcomes, time boundaries, cohorts, and stability checks from verified data documentation. | **Delivery Pattern Review:** identifies a supported pattern, affected context, evidence, limitations, and a proposed investigation. |
| Can potential fulfillment exceptions be identified early enough to support a useful operational response? | Compare validated candidate approaches against an appropriate baseline using leakage-safe temporal evaluation where applicable. | **Fulfillment Exception Alert:** describes the potential exception, timing, supporting signals, uncertainty, and a review action. |
| Which operational segments exhibit meaningfully different delivery behavior over time? | Define valid segments and compare outcome distributions, uncertainty, and temporal stability without assuming causation. | **Operational Segment Opportunity:** highlights a segment difference and recommends a targeted diagnostic step. |

## Personalization and recommendation

| Future research question | Measurement approach to define after validation | Future Decision Action Card |
|---|---|---|
| Can a recommendation approach improve relevant product discovery compared with a defensible non-personalized baseline? | Select offline relevance and ranking measures, eligibility rules, and leakage-safe splits based on verified interaction semantics. | **Recommendation Opportunity:** presents the audience context, candidate strategy, comparative evidence, limitations, and a test action. |
| How does recommendation usefulness vary across eligible user or product activity contexts? | Evaluate agreed ranking measures by validated cohorts, including coverage and uncertainty checks. | **Personalization Coverage Gap:** identifies an underserved context and proposes an experiment or fallback strategy. |
| Can recommendations balance relevance with useful discovery without degrading the agreed primary objective? | Define relevance, discovery, diversity, and coverage measures with stakeholders, then compare candidate strategies. | **Discovery Strategy Test:** summarizes the trade-off and proposes a controlled validation. |

## Voice of customer

| Future research question | Measurement approach to define after validation | Future Decision Action Card |
|---|---|---|
| Can review sentiment and recurring issue themes be identified with sufficient consistency for human triage? | Create an approved annotation and review protocol, then assess agreement, error patterns, and stability on held-out examples. | **Customer Issue Theme:** names a supported theme, evidence, uncertainty, scope, and suggested owner action. |
| Which customer-reported issues appear to change in prevalence or severity over time? | Define valid time windows, theme denominators, severity criteria, and uncertainty-aware change checks. | **Emerging Customer Concern:** describes the observed change and recommends investigation or monitoring. |
| Can feedback be grouped into action-oriented themes without hiding important minority concerns? | Evaluate theme quality, coverage, coherence, and subgroup error patterns through quantitative checks and human review. | **Feedback Action Priority:** summarizes an actionable theme while preserving minority signals and limitations. |

## Mapping requirements

A future Decision Action Card must retain the originating capability, research question, evidence reference, evaluation context, limitations, and proposed human-owned action. Cards must not imply cross-dataset identity or evidence.
